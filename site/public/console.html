Stream your own Snowflake ledger into this page

This console reads what the bridge publishes for your workspace. The bridge runs next to your Snowflake account — on your laptop or any box you trust — and pushes PUBLIC.NEGOTIATION_LEDGER, NEGOTIATIONS and ORDERS up as they change. Approvals you click here go back down to PUBLIC.APPROVE_ORDER.

Your cost basis and price floor stay in Snowflake: the bridge publishes a floor-relative position for the gauge, not the number, unless you pass --include-floor. Full setup: bridge/README.md.
